Pair Journey Terms of Use

Version dated 21 November 2026

Pair Journey is a learning game for adults aged 18 and over. The provider is Victor Junior Olufunto Agbolade Odukoya, Pappelweg 4, 53177 Bonn, Germany. For account questions, subscription questions or problems with the app, contact support@victorodukoya.com.

Learning, your content and backups

You can study and create cards on your device for free without an account. The free app may show ads. You retain your rights to your own content. Only use material you are entitled to use with the feature you choose. When you back up content or use AI to edit it, we may process that content as needed to carry out your request.

Your cards and learning progress are saved on your device. Optional cloud backup is included with Pro and requires a signed-in account and an internet connection. Restoring a backup can replace the current data on your device. See the Pair Journey Privacy Policy for details.

What Pro includes

Pair Journey Pro is an automatically renewing subscription billed monthly. Pro removes ads and includes up to 150 AI generations per account for each paid monthly subscription period. The first allowance starts at purchase. Each confirmed monthly renewal starts a new allowance. Cancelling leaves the remaining allowance available until the paid period ends. Restoring a purchase does not refill it. A free account can try one AI deck with 30 pairs without starting a subscription. Purchases and AI require a signed-in Pair Journey account and an internet connection. The app asks for your permission before first sending content to the AI provider. Check generated learning material for errors before relying on it.

The store shows your price, billing period and any offer conditions before you confirm your purchase. The store where you subscribe handles payment and renewal under its purchase terms. The seller for your payment is identified at checkout and on your receipt. The licence listed in the App Store applies to the iOS app, with Apple’s standard licence applying in the absence of a custom licence.

Cancellation and restoring purchases

Open your Apple Account settings or “Payments & subscriptions” in Google Play. Under “Subscriptions”, choose Pair Journey and cancel renewal. The store shows when access ends. Uninstalling the app or deleting your Pair Journey account leaves your store subscription running until you cancel it. After reinstalling, sign in with your original Pair Journey account and store account and choose “Restore purchases” in the app.

Withdrawal, refunds and statutory rights

If you bought Pair Journey Pro through Google Play in Germany and wish to withdraw from the purchase, use the Google Play withdrawal form. Sign in with the Google Account you used for the purchase. You can also find the form in your Google Play order history. Your order number is in your receipt email.

See the Apple Media Services terms or the Google Play terms for the withdrawal period, conditions, contact and forms applicable to your purchase in your region. You can report an Apple purchase or request a Google Play refund. Cancelling stops future renewals. Exercising a right of withdrawal concerns a contract you already entered into. You can also contact us directly if you experience a problem with the app.

You retain your statutory rights, including remedies for faulty services, required updates, withdrawal and compensation. Mandatory consumer protections in your country of residence apply.

Kündigen und Käufe wiederherstellen

Öffne bei Apple die Einstellungen deines Apple Accounts oder bei Google Play „Zahlungen und Abos“. Wähle unter „Abos“ oder „Abonnements“ Pair Journey und kündige die Verlängerung.
