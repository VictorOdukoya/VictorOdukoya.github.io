Pair Journey Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

You can learn in Pair Journey without creating an account. This notice covers the Android and iOS apps and the online services used by the app. Pair Journey is intended for adults aged 18 and over.

The controller is Victor Junior Olufunto Agbolade Odukoya, Pappelweg 4, 53177 Bonn, Germany. For privacy requests, write to support@victorodukoya.com.

Learning on your device

Your decks, card texts, groups, mistakes, round history, progress and preferences are stored on your device. Practice and streak reminders use the device’s notification system. Opening a stored deck or playing a round offline does not send its contents to us. Device backups are subject to your operating system’s settings.

If you choose Google, YouTube or ChatGPT on a learning card, Pair Journey includes both sides of that card and its subject, if present, in the link. The selected provider receives that query when the link opens and handles it under its own privacy policy. This action is separate from generating a deck with OpenAI.

Signing in and backing up

Supabase manages accounts and sessions. Google or Apple sign-in supplies an account identifier and may supply an email address and name. Google may supply a profile-picture URL. Google’s sign-in service also processes technical information, including IP-derived region, identifiers and SDK usage, for authentication, security and service operation. On iOS, Google’s sign-in SDK lists a phone number as account-linked data it may process for app functionality. Pair Journey requests the basic Google profile and email scopes. Apple’s Hide My Email address works as your account email. Where an Apple authorization credential is available, our server stores it encrypted for revocation. The app keeps session information on your device so you stay signed in.

The first time you sign in with an email code, confirming it creates your account. Later codes sign you back in. You can also register with a password or reset it after confirming your email address. Supabase uses Resend to deliver these codes. Resend processes your email address, the message containing the code, and delivery information such as time and delivery status. Never send support your code or password.

If you use Pro cloud backup, Supabase stores a private snapshot under your account ID. It includes decks, groups, settings, learning states, achievements and activity history, including rounds and mistakes. Automatic backup can update this snapshot when enabled. A successful new backup replaces the previous cloud snapshot. The connection is encrypted in transit. The server can read the snapshot. Signing out keeps your local learning data on the device.

Generating or editing learning material with AI

Before your first AI request, Pair Journey asks for permission to send the material needed for that action to OpenAI through our server. A new deck uses your topic, instructions and language. Editing, expanding or preparing a lesson may also send the deck title, subject, selected terms, answers, explanations or examples. Avoid entering confidential or personal details. Results return to your device and can be included in a cloud backup.

Our server checks your account and tracks whether you have used your one free AI deck. For Pro, it checks your subscription and usage for the paid subscription month. It keeps account-linked request and quota records. The OpenAI request contains the learning material and generation instructions. Pair Journey excludes your sign-in credentials, account ID and billing ID from that request. It disables storage for later retrieval via the Responses API. OpenAI’s standard API abuse-monitoring logs can retain request or response content for up to 30 days, or longer where legally required. OpenAI API data controls

You can withdraw permission in Settings → Privacy. Withdrawal stops future AI requests until you consent again. OpenAI may process submitted material outside the European Economic Area (EEA).

Bei der KI-Erstellung erhält OpenAI das Thema und deine Vorgaben. Beim Bearbeiten können die benötigten Kartentexte, Erklärungen und Beispiele hinzukommen. Die Verarbeitung kann außerhalb des EWR stattfinden. Unter Einstellungen → Datenschutz kannst du die Einwilligung jederzeit für die Zukunft widerrufen.

Reporting AI-generated content

Before sending a report, you can see the excerpt it contains. Supabase stores that excerpt, the deck title and identifier, any selected card identifier, your reason and optional comment, app version, account ID and receipt time. Victor Odukoya reviews reports to investigate errors or inappropriate material. Successful account deletion also removes the account’s reports from the active database.

Vor dem Absenden siehst du den gemeldeten Ausschnitt. Supabase speichert ihn mit Deckname und Deck-ID, gegebenenfalls der Karten-ID, Meldegrund, optionalem Kommentar, App-Version, Konto-ID und Eingangszeitpunkt. Victor Odukoya prüft die Meldung. Bei erfolgreicher Kontolöschung werden die zugehörigen Meldungen aus der aktiven Datenbank entfernt.

Purchases

Apple or Google handles payment for Pro. RevenueCat processes the app user ID, store transaction and subscription history, product, store and entitlement status to validate purchases, restore access and provide subscription reporting. When you sign in, the subscription is associated with your Pair Journey account. Pair Journey does not receive your full card number. The store keeps its own transaction records.

Advertising and consent choices

Pair Journey uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to check which privacy choices are required. Google can process your IP address to determine an approximate region, your interaction with the form and technical performance data. Where available, open Settings → Ad Preferences to review or change your choice. Pro is ad-free. The app can still refresh your privacy options when you use Pro.

The ad-supported version requests non-personalized ads. After you decline consent, Google can still allow limited ads. If Google’s consent service does not permit an ad request, Pair Journey skips it. Google Mobile Ads can collect and share device identifiers, approximate location inferred from IP, ad views and interactions, and diagnostics for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. On iOS, Google also describes crash and performance data. How Google uses information from partner apps

Where data goes and how long it is kept

Our Supabase production project has its primary region in Frankfurt, Germany. Service operations and support can involve other locations. The sending domain is configured for Ireland. Resend stores email and delivery logs in the United States and describes a standard 30-day retention period for email and log data on its Free plan. Google, Apple, OpenAI and RevenueCat may also process information outside the EEA. Resend identifies the EU Standard Contractual Clauses among its transfer safeguards. The providers publish their transfer terms for Resend, Supabase, OpenAI and RevenueCat. You can ask us which safeguards apply to a particular transfer and how to obtain a copy. Resend’s processing and retention information

Your local data stays until you remove it, subject to device backups. The current cloud snapshot stays until replaced or deleted. The account, linked AI content reports, AI request reservations and quota counters are removed from the active service on successful account deletion. A monthly quota reset is separate from record deletion. Database recovery copies rotate separately and can temporarily contain earlier database records. Supabase database backups contain storage metadata, not the contents of the cloud snapshot. We keep support correspondence while needed to resolve a request, document its handling or meet applicable legal duties.

Deleting a backup, deleting an account or obtaining a copy

  • Delete only the cloud backup. Open Settings → Sync → Delete Cloud Data. Turn off automatic backup if you want the cloud copy to stay empty. You keep your account and local decks. If the control is unavailable, email a backup-only deletion request.
  • Delete your account. While signed in, open Settings → Sync → Delete account, or request deletion on the web. This removes the active cloud account, its backup, associated quotas and content reports. Devices retain local learning data. Removing the app is a separate action.
  • Purchases and subscriptions. Cancel any ongoing subscription separately in the store where you bought it. Deleting your Pair Journey account also removes its RevenueCat customer profile and the purchase history shown there. Apple and Google keep their own transaction records. If the app cannot complete deletion, try again or contact support.
  • Access or portability. Email support to request a copy of the personal data we hold. We may ask you to verify account ownership. Data stored only on your device is accessible on that device.

Your rights and the legal bases we use in the EEA

We process the data needed to supply the account, backup and subscription features you request to perform our contract (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)). We rely on your consent for sending material to OpenAI (Article 6(1)(a)). For security, fraud prevention, investigating content reports and resolving support issues, we rely on our legitimate interests in protecting users and operating the service (Article 6(1)(f)). When the law requires us to keep records or handle a rights request, we process the necessary information to meet that obligation (Article 6(1)(c)). Where device access or advertising requires consent, you can manage that choice in the privacy options. Declining an optional feature still allows local learning.

Depending on the circumstances, you can request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability, object to processing based on legitimate interests and withdraw consent for the future. You can also complain to a data protection authority, including the authority where you live or work. Contact support@victorodukoya.com to exercise your rights.
