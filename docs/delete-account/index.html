Account and data deletion

Delete your Pair Journey account

Delete your account here if you no longer have access to Pair Journey.

Delete your account online

Sign in with the same Google account you use for Pair Journey. We use this sign-in only to verify that you own the account being deleted. We never ask for your Google password.

Signed in as . This permanently deletes your Pair Journey cloud account, cloud backup, and associated server-side quota data.

Online sign-in is not configured yet. You can still email a deletion request below.

Need help signing in?

If you cannot use Google sign-in, email us from the address associated with your Pair Journey account. Include “delete my Pair Journey account” and whether you use Android or iOS. We may ask you to verify account ownership. Do not send passwords, sign-in codes, or payment details.

Email a deletion request

Or email support@victorodukoya.com.

What deletion means

  • Your Pair Journey sign-in account, cloud backup, and associated server-side quota data are deleted.
  • Catalogs and progress stored only on your device are not deleted by removing the cloud account.
  • Deleting your Pair Journey account does not cancel an Apple App Store or Google Play subscription. Cancel it separately in the store where you subscribed.

See the Pair Journey privacy policy for details about data handling and deletion.Contact support if you need help.
